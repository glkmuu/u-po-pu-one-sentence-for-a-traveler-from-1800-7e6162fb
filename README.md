This entry explains the modern internet to a person arriving from the year 1800 in a single sentence of 160 characters, using only things they already know: roads, a post office, a library, a town square, letters, and books. It pictures every road, post office, and library on earth joined into one great square, where any letter or book reaches anyone at once and anyone may answer back — one image that carries what the internet is (a joining of every road of messages and every shelf of knowledge) and what it does (instant, two-way reach to anyone). `main.py` writes that sentence, unchanged, to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

> Imagine every road, post office, and library on earth joined into one great square, where any letter or book reaches anyone at once, and anyone may answer back.

160 characters, single line, no trailing newline, UTF-8.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
